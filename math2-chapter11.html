MATHEMATICS 2

Sequences & Series

Chapter 11 · 25 Questions

1. An arithmetic sequence has first term 3 and common difference 4. What is its fourth term?

The terms are 3, 7, 11, 15. Therefore the fourth term is 15.

2. An arithmetic sequence has a₁ = 5 and d = −2. Find a₆.

a₆ = a₁ + 5d = 5 + 5(−2) = −5.

3. If a₁ = 2 and d = 3 in an arithmetic sequence, find a₁₀.

a₁₀ = 2 + 9(3) = 29.

4. Find the sum of the first 10 positive integers.

1 + 2 + … + 10 = 10(11)/2 = 55.

5. A geometric sequence has first term 3 and ratio 2. Find its fifth term.

a₅ = 3·2⁴ = 3·16 = 48.

6. What is the common ratio of 2, 6, 18, …?

Each term is multiplied by 3.

7. Find 1 + 2 + 4 + 8.

Adding the four terms gives 15.

8. An arithmetic sequence has a₁ = 4 and d = 4. What is S₅?

The first five terms are 4, 8, 12, 16, 20; their sum is 60.

9. If aₙ = 2n + 1, what is a₇?

a₇ = 2(7) + 1 = 15.

10. If aₙ = n², what is a₅?

a₅ = 5² = 25.

11. In an arithmetic sequence, a₃ = 10 and a₇ = 22. What is d?

a₇ − a₃ covers four differences: 22 − 10 = 4d. Thus d = 3.

12. In a geometric sequence, a₂ = 6 and a₃ = 18. What is a₁?

The ratio is 18/6 = 3. Therefore a₁ = 6/3 = 2.

13. Find the sum to infinity of 4 + 2 + 1 + …

This geometric series has a = 4 and r = 1/2. S∞ = a/(1 − r) = 4/(1/2) = 8.

14. When does an infinite geometric series converge?

An infinite geometric series converges exactly when the absolute value of its ratio is less than 1.

15. Which formula gives the sum of the first n terms of an arithmetic sequence?

The arithmetic-series sum formula is Sₙ = n[2a₁ + (n − 1)d]/2.

16. For r ≠ 1, which formula gives the sum of the first n terms of a geometric sequence?

The finite geometric-series formula is a₁(rⁿ − 1)/(r − 1).

17. In the arithmetic sequence 7, 12, 17, …, which term is 32?

The terms are 7, 12, 17, 22, 27, 32. Thus 32 is the sixth term.

18. What is the sixth term of 81, 27, 9, …?

The ratio is 1/3. The terms continue 81, 27, 9, 3, 1, 1/3.

19. Find 1 + 1/2 + 1/4.

Using denominator 4: 4/4 + 2/4 + 1/4 = 7/4.

20. An arithmetic sequence has a₁ = 10 and d = 5. Which term is 35?

10 + (n − 1)5 = 35 gives 5(n − 1) = 25, so n = 6.

21. Find the sum of the first three terms of the geometric sequence with a₁ = 5 and r = 2.

The terms are 5, 10, 20; their sum is 35.

22. If Sₙ = n(n + 1)/2 equals 28, find n.

n(n + 1)/2 = 28 gives n(n + 1) = 56. The positive solution is n = 7.

23. Find 3 + 6 + 9 + … + 30.

There are 10 terms. S = 10(3 + 30)/2 = 165.

24. If aₙ = 4·3ⁿ⁻¹, find a₄.

a₄ = 4·3³ = 4·27 = 108.

25. Find the sum of the first 20 terms of 2, 5, 8, …

a₁ = 2, d = 3, and a₂₀ = 2 + 19·3 = 59. S₂₀ = 20(2 + 59)/2 = 610.
