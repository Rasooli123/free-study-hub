MATHEMATICS 2

Quadratic Inequalities

Chapter 3 · 25 Questions

1. Solve x² − 5x + 6 > 0.

x² − 5x + 6 = (x − 2)(x − 3). A positive-leading parabola is positive outside its roots.

2. Solve x² − 4x − 5 ≤ 0.

The roots are −1 and 5. The parabola opens upward, so it is non-positive between and including the roots.

3. Solve x² − 9 < 0.

x² − 9 = (x − 3)(x + 3). It is negative strictly between −3 and 3.

4. Solve (x − 2)(x + 1) ≥ 0.

The product is non-negative outside the roots −1 and 2, including the roots.

5. For which x is x² + 2x + 1 > 0?

x² + 2x + 1 = (x + 1)². A square is positive except when it equals 0, at x = −1.

6. Solve x² − 6x + 8 < 0.

Factor as (x − 2)(x − 4). It is negative between the roots.

7. Solve 2x² − 8x ≥ 0.

2x² − 8x = 2x(x − 4). With positive leading coefficient, the product is non-negative outside 0 and 4.

8. Solve −x² + 4x + 5 > 0.

Multiplying by −1 reverses the inequality: x² − 4x − 5 < 0. Its roots are −1 and 5, so the solution is between them.

9. Solve x² + x − 6 ≤ 0.

x² + x − 6 = (x + 3)(x − 2). It is non-positive from −3 to 2.

10. Solve x² − 2x − 3 > 0.

The roots are −1 and 3. An upward parabola is positive outside those roots.

11. Solve x² + 4x + 4 ≤ 0.

x² + 4x + 4 = (x + 2)². A square is at most 0 only when it is 0.

12. Solve (x − 1)(x − 5) < 0.

A product of these two linear factors is negative between the roots 1 and 5.

13. Solve x² − 16 ≥ 0.

x² − 16 = (x − 4)(x + 4). It is non-negative outside the roots.

14. Solve x² + 3x + 2 < 0.

Factor as (x + 2)(x + 1). The upward parabola is negative between −2 and −1.

15. Solve 3x² − 12x + 9 ≥ 0.

3x² − 12x + 9 = 3(x − 1)(x − 3). The positive factor 3 does not change the sign.

16. Solve x² − 7x + 12 ≤ 0.

(x − 3)(x − 4) ≤ 0, which holds between the roots including endpoints.

17. Solve −2x² + 8x ≤ 0.

Divide by −2 and reverse the sign: x(x − 4) ≥ 0. This holds outside 0 and 4.

18. Solve x² + 1 > 0.

For every real x, x² ≥ 0, so x² + 1 is always positive.

19. Solve (x + 2)² ≥ 0.

Every real square is greater than or equal to 0.

20. Solve (x + 2)² < 0.

A real square can never be negative.

21. For which x is x² − 10x + 25 > 0?

x² − 10x + 25 = (x − 5)², which is positive for every x other than 5.

22. Solve x² − x − 6 ≥ 0.

(x − 3)(x + 2) ≥ 0. The solution is outside the two roots.

23. Solve −x² + 9 ≥ 0.

−x² + 9 ≥ 0 means x² ≤ 9, so −3 ≤ x ≤ 3.

24. Solve 4x² − 1 < 0.

4x² − 1 = (2x − 1)(2x + 1). It is negative between −1/2 and 1/2.

25. Solve (2x − 3)(x + 1) > 0.

The roots are −1 and 3/2. Since the leading coefficient is positive, the product is positive outside the roots.
