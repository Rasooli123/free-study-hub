MATHEMATICS 1

Modular Arithmetic

Chapter 14 · 25 Questions

1. What is the remainder when 17 is divided by 5?

17 = 5 × 3 + 2. Therefore, the remainder is 2.

2. What is the remainder when 29 is divided by 6?

29 = 6 × 4 + 5. Therefore, the remainder is 5.

3. Which number is congruent to 4 modulo 7?

11 = 7 + 4. Therefore, 11 leaves remainder 4 when divided by 7.

4. What is the remainder when 45 is divided by 8?

45 = 8 × 5 + 5. Therefore, the remainder is 5.

5. If a number leaves remainder 3 when divided by 7, what remainder will the number + 5 leave when divided by 7?

The original remainder is 3. Adding 5 gives 8. Since 8 leaves remainder 1 when divided by 7, the answer is 1.

6. If n ≡ 2 (mod 5), what is the remainder of n + 7 when divided by 5?

n has remainder 2. 7 has remainder 2 modulo 5. Therefore, 2 + 2 = 4. The remainder is 4.

7. What is the remainder when 38 + 27 is divided by 5?

38 + 27 = 65. Since 65 is divisible by 5, the remainder is 0.

8. What is the remainder when 7 × 13 is divided by 5?

7 × 13 = 91. 91 = 5 × 18 + 1. Therefore, the remainder is 1.

9. If n ≡ 4 (mod 6), which of the following could be n?

16 = 6 × 2 + 4. Therefore, 16 has remainder 4 when divided by 6.

10. What is the remainder when 2² + 3² is divided by 5?

2² + 3² = 4 + 9 = 13. 13 leaves remainder 3 when divided by 5.

11. What is the remainder when 3⁴ is divided by 5?

3⁴ = 81. 81 = 5 × 16 + 1. Therefore, the remainder is 1.

12. If n ≡ 5 (mod 8), what is the remainder of 3n when divided by 8?

Since n has remainder 5, 3n has the same remainder as 3 × 5 = 15. 15 leaves remainder 7 when divided by 8.

13. What is the remainder when 10² + 10³ is divided by 9?

10 ≡ 1 (mod 9). Therefore: 10² + 10³ ≡ 1² + 1³ = 2 (mod 9).

14. A number gives remainder 2 when divided by 4. What remainder does its square give when divided by 4?

If n ≡ 2 (mod 4), then: n² ≡ 2² = 4 ≡ 0 (mod 4). Therefore, the remainder is 0.

15. What is the remainder when 2⁵ is divided by 7?

2⁵ = 32. 32 = 7 × 4 + 4. Therefore, the remainder is 4.

16. If n ≡ 3 (mod 7), what is the remainder of n² + 2n when divided by 7?

Substitute the remainder 3: n² + 2n ≡ 3² + 2(3) = 9 + 6 = 15. 15 leaves remainder 1 when divided by 7.

17. What is the remainder when 4³ + 5 is divided by 7?

4³ = 64. 64 + 5 = 69. Since 69 = 7 × 9 + 6, the remainder is 6.

18. If n leaves remainder 4 when divided by 9, what remainder does n + 14 leave when divided by 9?

n has remainder 4. 14 has remainder 5 modulo 9. Therefore: 4 + 5 = 9, which gives remainder 0.

19. What is the remainder when 2⁶ is divided by 9?

2⁶ = 64. 64 = 9 × 7 + 1. Therefore, the remainder is 1.

20. A number is of the form 5k + 3. What is its remainder when divided by 5?

5k is divisible by 5. Therefore, 5k + 3 always leaves remainder 3.

21. If a ≡ 2 (mod 5) and b ≡ 4 (mod 5), what is the remainder of ab when divided by 5?

ab ≡ 2 × 4 = 8 (mod 5). Since 8 leaves remainder 3, the answer is 3.

22. What is the remainder when 7⁴ is divided by 6?

7 ≡ 1 (mod 6). Therefore: 7⁴ ≡ 1⁴ = 1 (mod 6).

23. If n ≡ 5 (mod 6), what is the remainder of n² − n when divided by 6?

Substitute n ≡ 5: n² − n ≡ 5² − 5 = 25 − 5 = 20. 20 leaves remainder 2 when divided by 6.

24. What is the smallest positive integer x such that x ≡ 17 (mod 6)?

17 divided by 6 leaves remainder 5. Therefore, the smallest positive integer congruent to 17 modulo 6 is 5.

25. What is the remainder when 3⁵ + 2⁵ is divided by 5?

3⁵ = 243 and 2⁵ = 32. Their sum is 275. Since 275 is divisible by 5, the remainder is 0.
