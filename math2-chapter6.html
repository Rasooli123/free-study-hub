MATHEMATICS 2

Complex Numbers

Chapter 6 · 25 Questions

1. What is i²?

By definition, i² = −1.

2. What is i³?

i³ = i²·i = (−1)i = −i.

3. What is i⁴?

i⁴ = (i²)² = (−1)² = 1.

4. Simplify (3 + 2i) + (1 − 5i).

Add real and imaginary parts separately: 3 + 1 = 4 and 2i − 5i = −3i.

5. Simplify (4 + 3i) − (1 + 7i).

Subtract corresponding parts: 4 − 1 = 3 and 3i − 7i = −4i.

6. Simplify (2 + i)(3 − 2i).

Multiply: 6 − 4i + 3i − 2i² = 6 − i + 2 = 8 − i.

7. Simplify (1 + i)².

(1 + i)² = 1 + 2i + i² = 2i.

8. Simplify (3 + 4i)(3 − 4i).

Conjugates multiply to a² + b²: 3² + 4² = 25.

9. What is the conjugate of −2 + 5i?

The conjugate keeps the real part and changes the sign of the imaginary part.

10. What is |3 − 4i|?

The modulus is √(3² + (−4)²) = √25 = 5.

11. What is the real part of 7 − 3i?

The real part is the non-imaginary component, 7.

12. What is the imaginary part of −1 + 6i?

The imaginary part is the coefficient of i, which is 6.

13. Simplify (4 + 2i)/(1 + i).

Multiply by (1 − i): [(4 + 2i)(1 − i)]/2 = (6 − 2i)/2 = 3 − i.

14. If z + (2 − 3i) = 5 + i, find z.

Subtract 2 − 3i: z = (5 + i) − (2 − 3i) = 3 + 4i.

15. What is i¹⁷?

Powers of i repeat every four. Since 17 leaves remainder 1 when divided by 4, i¹⁷ = i.

16. What is i²⁰?

20 is divisible by 4, so i²⁰ = 1.

17. Simplify (2 − i)(2 + i).

This is a product of conjugates: 2² + 1² = 5.

18. Simplify (1 − i)³.

(1 − i)² = −2i; multiplying by (1 − i) gives −2i + 2i² = −2 − 2i.

19. If z = 2 + 3i, what is |z|²?

|z|² = 2² + 3² = 13.

20. What are the complex roots of x² + 1 = 0?

x² = −1, so x = ±i.

21. Simplify 1/i.

Multiply by i/i: 1/i = i/i² = i/(−1) = −i.

22. Simplify (3 + i) + (−3 − i).

Both the real and imaginary parts cancel, giving 0.

23. What is i³⁵?

35 leaves remainder 3 after division by 4, so i³⁵ = i³ = −i.

24. Simplify (2 + 3i)².

(2 + 3i)² = 4 + 12i + 9i² = −5 + 12i.

25. What is |1 − √3i|?

The modulus is √(1² + (√3)²) = √4 = 2.
