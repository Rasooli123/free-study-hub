Chapter 6: Factoring

Practice questions based on the chapter.

1. Factor completely: 6x + 12

The greatest common factor of 6x and 12 is 6. Therefore, 6x + 12 = 6(x + 2).

2. Factor: x² + 5x + 6

We need two numbers whose product is 6 and whose sum is 5. They are 2 and 3. Therefore, x² + 5x + 6 = (x + 2)(x + 3).

3. Factor: x² − 9

x² − 9 is a difference of squares: x² − 3² = (x − 3)(x + 3).

4. Factor: x² − 7x + 12

We need two numbers with product 12 and sum −7. They are −3 and −4. Therefore, the factorization is (x − 3)(x − 4).

5. Factor completely: 4x² + 12x

The greatest common factor is 4x. Thus, 4x² + 12x = 4x(x + 3).

6. Which is the complete factorization of 9x² − 25?

9x² − 25 = (3x)² − 5². Using the difference of squares: (3x − 5)(3x + 5).

7. Factor: x² + 10x + 25

x² + 10x + 25 = x² + 2(5)x + 5², so it is the perfect square (x + 5)².

8. Factor: x² − 12x + 36

x² − 12x + 36 = x² − 2(6)x + 6², so the factorization is (x − 6)².

9. Factor: 2x² + 7x + 3

(2x + 1)(x + 3) = 2x² + 6x + x + 3 = 2x² + 7x + 3.

10. If x² − 16 = 0, which values can x have?

x² − 16 = (x − 4)(x + 4) = 0. Therefore, x = 4 or x = −4.

11. Factor: 3x² − 12

First take out 3: 3(x² − 4). Then factor the difference of squares: 3(x − 2)(x + 2).

12. Which expression is NOT factorable over the integers?

x² + 2x + 2 cannot be factored into linear factors with integer coefficients. Its discriminant is 2² − 4(1)(2) = −4.

13. Factor: x³ + 3x²

The greatest common factor is x²: x³ + 3x² = x²(x + 3).

14. Factor: x² − x − 12

We need two numbers whose product is −12 and whose sum is −1. They are −4 and 3. Therefore, x² − x − 12 = (x − 4)(x + 3).

15. Factor: 4x² − 12x + 9

4x² − 12x + 9 = (2x)² − 2(2x)(3) + 3² = (2x − 3)².

16. If x² − 5x + 6 = 0, what is the sum of the solutions?

x² − 5x + 6 = (x − 2)(x − 3). The solutions are 2 and 3, whose sum is 5.

17. Factor completely: 6x² + 15x

The greatest common factor is 3x: 6x² + 15x = 3x(2x + 5).

18. Factor by grouping: x³ + 2x² + 3x + 6

Group the terms: x²(x + 2) + 3(x + 2). Taking out the common factor (x + 2) gives (x + 2)(x² + 3).

19. If x² − 10x + 21 = 0, what is the larger solution?

x² − 10x + 21 = (x − 3)(x − 7). The solutions are 3 and 7, so the larger solution is 7.

20. Factor completely: 2x² − 8x − 24

First take out 2: 2(x² − 4x − 12). Then x² − 4x − 12 = (x − 6)(x + 2). Therefore, the complete factorization is 2(x − 6)(x + 2).

21. Which expression is equivalent to (x + 4)² − 16?

Expand: (x + 4)² − 16 = x² + 8x + 16 − 16 = x² + 8x = x(x + 8).

22. If (x − 5)(x + 2) = 0, what is the sum of the solutions?

The solutions are x = 5 and x = −2. Their sum is 5 + (−2) = 3.

23. Factor: 3x² + 11x + 6

(3x + 2)(x + 3) = 3x² + 9x + 2x + 6 = 3x² + 11x + 6.

24. If x² − 4x = 0, what is the product of the solutions?

x² − 4x = x(x − 4) = 0. The solutions are 0 and 4. Their product is 0.

25. If x² − 9x + 20 = 0, what is the smaller solution?

x² − 9x + 20 = (x − 4)(x − 5). The solutions are 4 and 5, so the smaller solution is 4.
