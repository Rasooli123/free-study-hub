MATHEMATICS 2

Integrals

Chapter 13 · 25 Questions

1. Find ∫x² dx.

Increase the exponent by 1 and divide by it: ∫x² dx = x³/3 + C.

2. Find ∫3x² dx.

Since d/dx(x³) = 3x², the integral is x³ + C.

3. Find ∫5 dx.

The integral of a constant k is kx + C.

4. Find ∫(2x + 3) dx.

Integrate term by term: ∫2x dx = x² and ∫3 dx = 3x.

5. Find ∫x⁻² dx.

The power rule gives x⁻¹/(−1) = −x⁻¹ + C.

6. Find ∫(1/x) dx.

The special antiderivative of 1/x is ln|x| + C.

7. Find ∫cos x dx.

The derivative of sin x is cos x.

8. Find ∫sin x dx.

Because d/dx(cos x) = −sin x, the antiderivative is −cos x + C.

9. Find ∫eˣ dx.

eˣ is its own derivative and antiderivative.

10. Find ∫(4x³ − 2x) dx.

Integrate each term: x⁴ − x² + C.

11. Find ∫₀¹ x dx.

An antiderivative is x²/2. Evaluating from 0 to 1 gives 1/2.

12. Find ∫₁³ 2x dx.

An antiderivative is x². Thus 3² − 1² = 9 − 1 = 8.

13. Find ∫₀^π sin x dx.

[−cos x]₀^π = −cos π − (−cos 0) = 1 + 1 = 2.

14. Find ∫₀^(π/2) cos x dx.

[sin x]₀^(π/2) = 1 − 0 = 1.

15. Find ∫(3x² + 4) dx.

∫3x² dx = x³ and ∫4 dx = 4x.

16. If F′(x) = 2x and F(0) = 5, what is F(x)?

An antiderivative of 2x is x² + C. F(0) = 5 gives C = 5.

17. Find ∫(x + 1)² dx.

Let u = x + 1; then ∫u² du = u³/3 + C.

18. Find ∫1/√x dx.

1/√x = x⁻¹ᐟ². Its integral is x¹ᐟ²/(1/2) = 2√x + C.

19. Find ∫₀² x² dx.

[x³/3]₀² = 8/3.

20. Find ∫₋₁¹ x³ dx.

x³ is an odd function, so its integral over symmetric limits −1 to 1 is 0.

21. Find ∫₀¹ (x² + 1) dx.

[x³/3 + x]₀¹ = 1/3 + 1 = 4/3.

22. Find ∫(2/x) dx.

The constant factor remains: 2∫(1/x)dx = 2ln|x| + C.

23. Find ∫sec²x dx.

The derivative of tan x is sec²x.

24. Find the area under y = 2x from x = 0 to x = 3.

The area is ∫₀³ 2x dx = [x²]₀³ = 9.

25. Which function is an antiderivative of 6x − 4?

Differentiating 3x² − 4x + C gives 6x − 4.
