Chapter 10: Ratio & Proportion

25 TR-YÖS Practice Questions

1. What is the simplest form of the ratio 24 : 36?

Divide both numbers by their greatest common divisor, 12: 24 ÷ 12 = 2 and 36 ÷ 12 = 3. Therefore, the simplified ratio is 2 : 3.

2. If a : b = 3 : 5 and b = 20, what is a?

Since b = 20 corresponds to 5 parts, one part is 20 ÷ 5 = 4. Therefore, a = 3 × 4 = 12.

3. If x : 18 = 5 : 6, what is x?

x / 18 = 5 / 6. Therefore, 6x = 90, so x = 15.

4. If 7 : x = 14 : 18, what is x?

7/x = 14/18. Cross multiplication gives 126 = 14x. Therefore, x = 9.

5. The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 3 : 4. If there are 28 girls, how many students are there in total?

4 parts represent 28 girls, so one part is 7. Boys = 3 × 7 = 21. Total students = 21 + 28 = 49.

6. Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7. If their sum is 72, what is the larger number?

The total number of parts is 5 + 7 = 12. One part is 72 ÷ 12 = 6. The larger number is 7 × 6 = 42.

7. If a : b = 4 : 7 and b : c = 14 : 15, what is a : c?

Convert a : b = 4 : 7 to 8 : 14 so that the b terms match. Since b : c = 14 : 15, we get a : c = 8 : 15.

8. If 6 workers complete a task in 15 days, how many days would 10 workers need, assuming they work at the same rate?

Workers and days are inversely proportional. Total work = 6 × 15 = 90 worker-days. Therefore, 10 workers need 90 ÷ 10 = 9 days.

9. If 8 notebooks cost $24, how much do 15 notebooks cost at the same price per notebook?

Each notebook costs 24 ÷ 8 = $3. Therefore, 15 notebooks cost 15 × 3 = $45.

10. A map has a scale of 1 : 200,000. If the distance between two cities on the map is 3 cm, what is the actual distance?

1 cm represents 200,000 cm. Therefore, 3 cm represents 600,000 cm. Since 100,000 cm = 1 km, the actual distance is 6 km.

11. The ratio of red balls to blue balls is 5 : 3. If there are 64 balls in total, how many are red?

There are 5 + 3 = 8 total parts. Each part is 64 ÷ 8 = 8. Red balls = 5 × 8 = 40.

12. If x : y = 2 : 3 and y : z = 4 : 5, what is x : z?

Make the y terms equal. x : y = 2 : 3 becomes 8 : 12. y : z = 4 : 5 becomes 12 : 15. Therefore, x : z = 8 : 15.

13. If 12 machines produce 360 units in one hour, how many units will 20 machines produce in one hour at the same rate?

One machine produces 360 ÷ 12 = 30 units per hour. Therefore, 20 machines produce 20 × 30 = 600 units.

14. A quantity is divided into two parts in the ratio 7 : 5. If the larger part is 42, what is the smaller part?

7 parts correspond to 42, so one part is 6. The smaller part is 5 × 6 = 30.

15. If a : b = 3 : 4, what is the ratio (2a + b) : (a + 2b)?

Let a = 3k and b = 4k. Then 2a + b = 6k + 4k = 10k. Also, a + 2b = 3k + 8k = 11k. Therefore, the ratio is 10 : 11.

16. If 4 : x = x : 9 and x is positive, what is x?

4/x = x/9. Cross multiplication gives x² = 36. Since x is positive, x = 6.

17. The ratio of the ages of A and B is 3 : 5. If their total age is 48, what is B's age?

The total number of parts is 3 + 5 = 8. One part is 48 ÷ 8 = 6. B's age is 5 × 6 = 30.

18. If x varies directly with y and x = 12 when y = 4, what is x when y = 10?

Since x varies directly with y, x/y is constant. 12/4 = 3, so x = 3 × 10 = 30.

19. If x varies inversely with y and x = 8 when y = 6, what is x when y = 12?

For inverse variation, xy is constant. Initially, 8 × 6 = 48. Therefore, x × 12 = 48, giving x = 4.

20. A recipe uses flour and sugar in the ratio 5 : 2. If 600 g of flour is used, how much sugar is needed?

5 parts of flour correspond to 600 g, so one part is 120 g. Sugar represents 2 parts: 2 × 120 = 240 g.

21. The ratio of A to B is 4 : 7. If 6 is added to both A and B, the new ratio becomes 5 : 8. What is A?

Let A = 4k and B = 7k. After adding 6: (4k + 6)/(7k + 6) = 5/8. Cross multiplication gives: 32k + 48 = 35k + 30. Thus 18 = 3k, so k = 6. Therefore, A = 4 × 6 = 24.

22. Three numbers are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. If the difference between the largest and smallest is 36, what is the sum of the three numbers?

Let the numbers be 2k, 3k and 5k. Their difference is 5k − 2k = 3k = 36, so k = 12. Their sum is (2 + 3 + 5) × 12 = 120.

23. A sum of money is divided among A, B and C in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. If C receives $120 more than A, what is the total amount?

C − A corresponds to 5 − 2 = 3 parts. These 3 parts equal $120, so one part is $40. The total is 2 + 3 + 5 = 10 parts: 10 × $40 = $400.

24. If a : b = 2 : 3 and b : c = 5 : 7, what is (a + b) : (b + c)?

Make b equal in both ratios. a : b = 2 : 3 becomes 10 : 15. b : c = 5 : 7 becomes 15 : 21. Therefore: a + b = 10 + 15 = 25, and b + c = 15 + 21 = 36. So the ratio is 25 : 36.

25. Two positive numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. If 8 is added to each number, their ratio becomes 5 : 7. What is the smaller number?

Let the numbers be 3k and 5k. After adding 8: (3k + 8)/(5k + 8) = 5/7. Cross multiplication gives: 21k + 56 = 25k + 40. Therefore, 16 = 4k, so k = 4. The smaller number is 3 × 4 = 12.
