MATHEMATICS 2

Derivatives

Chapter 12 · 25 Questions

1. Find d/dx (x⁵).

By the power rule, d/dx(x⁵) = 5x⁴.

2. Find d/dx (3x²).

The power rule gives 3·2x = 6x.

3. Find d/dx (7).

The derivative of a constant is 0.

4. Find d/dx (x³ − 4x).

Differentiate term by term: 3x² − 4.

5. Find d/dx (5x⁴ − 2x² + 9).

The derivative is 20x³ − 4x; the constant 9 disappears.

6. Find d/dx (x⁻²).

The power rule gives −2x⁻³.

7. Find d/dx (√x).

√x = x¹ᐟ², so its derivative is (1/2)x⁻¹ᐟ² = 1/(2√x).

8. Find d/dx [(x² + 1)²].

Use the chain rule: 2(x² + 1) multiplied by the inner derivative 2x.

9. Find d/dx [(3x − 2)³].

Chain rule: 3(3x − 2)²·3 = 9(3x − 2)².

10. Find d/dx (sin x).

The derivative of sin x is cos x.

11. Find d/dx (cos x).

The derivative of cos x is −sin x.

12. Find d/dx (tan x).

The derivative of tan x is sec²x.

13. Find d/dx (eˣ).

The exponential function eˣ is its own derivative.

14. Find d/dx (ln x).

For x > 0, the derivative of ln x is 1/x.

15. If f(x) = x², find f′(3).

f′(x) = 2x, so f′(3) = 6.

16. What is the slope of the tangent to y = x² at x = 2?

The derivative is 2x. At x = 2, the slope is 4.

17. What is the slope of the tangent to y = x³ at x = 2?

y′ = 3x². At x = 2, the slope is 3·4 = 12.

18. Find d/dx [x(x + 1)].

First expand to x² + x, whose derivative is 2x + 1.

19. Find d/dx [(x² + 1)/x].

Rewrite as x + 1/x. Its derivative is 1 − 1/x².

20. Find d/dx [(x² − 1)(x + 2)].

Product rule gives 2x(x + 2) + (x² − 1) = 3x² + 4x − 1.

21. Find d/dx (1/x).

1/x = x⁻¹, so its derivative is −x⁻² = −1/x².

22. Find d/dx [sin(2x)].

By the chain rule, multiply cos(2x) by the derivative of 2x, which is 2.

23. Find d/dx [cos(3x)].

Differentiate cos to −sin, then multiply by the inner derivative 3.

24. Find d/dx [(x³ + 1)²].

Chain rule: 2(x³ + 1)·3x² = 6x²(x³ + 1).

25. For f(x) = x² − 4x, where is f′(x) = 0?

f′(x) = 2x − 4. Setting it equal to 0 gives x = 2.
