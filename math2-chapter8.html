MATHEMATICS 2

Summation & Product Symbols

Chapter 8 · 25 Questions

1. Find ∑ₖ₌₁⁴ k.

1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10.

2. Find ∑ₖ₌₁⁵ 2k.

2(1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5) = 2·15 = 30.

3. Find ∏ₖ₌₁⁴ k.

The product is 1·2·3·4 = 24.

4. Find ∑ₖ₌₁³ (k + 1).

The terms are 2, 3, and 4; their sum is 9.

5. Find ∑ₖ₌₀³ 2ᵏ.

The sum is 2⁰ + 2¹ + 2² + 2³ = 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 = 15.

6. Which formula equals ∑ₖ₌₁ⁿ k?

The sum of the first n positive integers is n(n + 1)/2.

7. Find ∑ₖ₌₁⁴ k².

1² + 2² + 3² + 4² = 1 + 4 + 9 + 16 = 30.

8. Find ∏ₖ₌₁³ (k + 1).

The product is 2·3·4 = 24.

9. Find ∑ₖ₌₁⁵ 1.

There are five terms, each equal to 1, so the total is 5.

10. Find ∑ₖ₌₁³ (2k − 1).

The terms are 1, 3, and 5; their sum is 9.

11. Find ∑ᵣ₌₂⁴ r.

2 + 3 + 4 = 9.

12. Find ∑ₖ₌₁⁴ (3 − k).

The terms are 2, 1, 0, and −1. Their sum is 2.

13. Find ∏ₖ₌₂⁴ k.

2·3·4 = 24.

14. Find ∑ₖ₌₁³ k(k + 1).

The terms are 2, 6, and 12, giving 20.

15. Find ∑ₖ₌₁⁴ (−1)ᵏ.

The terms are −1, 1, −1, 1, so they cancel to 0.

16. Find ∑ₖ₌₀² (k² + 1).

For k = 0, 1, 2 the terms are 1, 2, 5. Their sum is 8.

17. Find ∏ₖ₌₁³ 2.

The constant 2 is multiplied three times: 2·2·2 = 8.

18. Find ∑ₖ₌₁⁵ (k − 2).

∑k = 15 and ∑2 over five terms is 10, so 15 − 10 = 5.

19. Find ∑ₖ₌₁³ 2k.

2 + 4 + 6 = 12.

20. Which expression equals ∑ₖ₌₁ⁿ (2k − 1)?

The sum of the first n odd numbers is n².

21. Find ∑ₖ₌₁⁴ 1/k.

1 + 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/4 = 12/12 + 6/12 + 4/12 + 3/12 = 25/12.

22. Find ∏ₖ₌₁⁴ k/(k + 1).

The product telescopes: (1/2)(2/3)(3/4)(4/5) = 1/5.

23. Find ∑ₖ₌₁³ 3ᵏ.

3 + 9 + 27 = 39.

24. Find ∑ₖ₌₁⁴ (k² − k).

The terms are 0, 2, 6, and 12, which sum to 20.

25. Find ∏ₖ₌₁³ (2k − 1).

The factors are 1, 3, and 5. Their product is 15.
