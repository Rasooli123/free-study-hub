MATHEMATICS 1

Chapter 2: Rational Numbers

Practice questions based on the chapter.

Question 1

Which of the following is a rational number?

3/5 is the ratio of two integers, so it is a rational number.

Question 2

Which fraction is equivalent to 2/3?

Multiplying both the numerator and denominator of 2/3 by 4 gives 8/12.

Question 3

What is the simplest form of 18/24?

Dividing both 18 and 24 by their greatest common divisor, 6, gives 3/4.

Question 4

Which fraction is greater?

Since all fractions have denominator 8, the largest numerator gives the largest fraction. Therefore, 5/8 is greatest.

Question 5

What is 1/2 + 1/3?

The common denominator is 6: 1/2 = 3/6 and 1/3 = 2/6. Therefore, 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

Question 6

What is 3/4 − 1/4?

The denominators are already equal, so subtract the numerators: 3/4 − 1/4 = 2/4 = 1/2.

Question 7

What is 2/3 × 9/4?

Multiply the numerators and denominators: (2 × 9)/(3 × 4) = 18/12 = 3/2.

Question 8

What is 3/5 ÷ 6/5?

Divide by multiplying by the reciprocal: 3/5 × 5/6 = 3/6 = 1/2.

Question 9

Which decimal is equal to 3/4?

3 ÷ 4 = 0.75, so 3/4 = 0.75.

Question 10

Which fraction represents 0.2?

0.2 = 2/10 = 1/5.

Question 11

What is 2 1/3 written as an improper fraction?

2 × 3 + 1 = 7, so 2 1/3 = 7/3.

Question 12

Which fraction is the smallest?

1/4 = 0.25, which is smaller than 1/2, 2/3, and 3/4.

Question 13

What is the reciprocal of 5/7?

The reciprocal is found by switching the numerator and denominator. Therefore, the reciprocal of 5/7 is 7/5.

Question 14

What is (-2/3) + (1/3)?

The denominators are equal: -2/3 + 1/3 = -1/3.

Question 15

What is (-3/4) × (8/9)?

(-3 × 8)/(4 × 9) = -24/36 = -2/3.

Question 16

What is 5/6 − 1/3?

1/3 = 2/6, so 5/6 − 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2.

Question 17

Which of the following is between 1/3 and 1/2?

1/3 ≈ 0.333, 2/5 = 0.4, and 1/2 = 0.5. Therefore, 2/5 lies between them.

Question 18

What is 1 − 3/8?

1 = 8/8, so 8/8 − 3/8 = 5/8.

Question 19

What is 4/5 ÷ 2/3?

4/5 ÷ 2/3 = 4/5 × 3/2 = 12/10 = 6/5.

Question 20

Which of the following is equal to -0.75?

0.75 = 3/4, so -0.75 = -3/4.

Question 21

If x = 2/3, what is 3x?

3 × 2/3 = 6/3 = 2.

Question 22

Which fraction is closest to 1?

9/10 = 0.9, which is only 0.1 away from 1 and is therefore the closest.

Question 23

What is the value of (2/3)²?

Square both numerator and denominator: (2/3)² = 4/9.

Question 24

If a/b = 3/4 and b = 20, what is a?

a/20 = 3/4. Therefore, a = 20 × 3/4 = 15.

Question 25

What is the value of 1/(1/2)?

Dividing 1 by 1/2 is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal: 1 × 2 = 2.
