MATHEMATICS 2

Quadratic Equations

Chapter 2 · 25 Questions

1. Solve x² − 5x + 6 = 0.

x² − 5x + 6 = (x − 2)(x − 3). Thus x = 2 or x = 3.

2. What are the roots of x² − 9 = 0?

x² = 9, so x = ±3. The roots are −3 and 3.

3. What is the discriminant of x² + 4x + 5 = 0?

For ax² + bx + c, the discriminant is b² − 4ac. Here it is 4² − 4(1)(5) = −4.

4. What is the sum of the roots of x² − 7x + 10 = 0?

The sum of roots is −b/a. Here −(−7)/1 = 7.

5. What is the product of the roots of 2x² − 3x − 5 = 0?

The product of roots is c/a = −5/2.

6. Solve 3x² − 12x = 0.

Factor: 3x(x − 4) = 0. Therefore x = 0 or x = 4.

7. Which equation has roots 1 and −4?

The equation is (x − 1)(x + 4) = x² + 3x − 4 = 0.

8. Solve x² = 7.

Both √7 and −√7 have square 7, so x = ±√7.

9. Solve x² + 2x − 8 = 0.

x² + 2x − 8 = (x + 4)(x − 2). Thus x = −4 or 2.

10. Which value is the repeated root of x² − 6x + 9 = 0?

x² − 6x + 9 = (x − 3)², so the repeated root is 3.

11. How many real roots does x² + 1 = 0 have?

Its discriminant is 0² − 4(1)(1) = −4, which is negative. Therefore there are no real roots.

12. Solve 2x² + x − 3 = 0.

Factor as (2x + 3)(x − 1) = 0. Hence x = −3/2 or 1.

13. The roots of an equation have sum 8. If one root is 5, what is the other?

The other root is 8 − 5 = 3.

14. If the roots are −2 and 5, what is their product?

(−2)(5) = −10.

15. What is the positive root of x² − 4x − 12 = 0?

(x − 6)(x + 2) = 0, so the roots are 6 and −2. The positive root is 6.

16. Solve 2x² − 8 = 0.

2x² = 8 gives x² = 4, so x = ±2.

17. What is the discriminant of 4x² − 4x + 1 = 0?

b² − 4ac = (−4)² − 4(4)(1) = 16 − 16 = 0.

18. What is the larger root of x² − x − 1 = 0?

Using the quadratic formula gives x = [1 ± √5]/2. The larger value uses the plus sign.

19. Solve (x + 3)² = 16.

x + 3 = ±4. Therefore x = 1 or x = −7.

20. Solve x(x − 7) = 18.

x² − 7x − 18 = 0 = (x − 9)(x + 2). Thus x = 9 or −2.

21. Which equation has root sum −7 and root product 12?

An equation with sum S and product P is x² − Sx + P = 0. With S = −7 and P = 12, it is x² + 7x + 12 = 0.

22. If x = 2 is a root of x² + kx − 8 = 0, find k.

Substitute x = 2: 4 + 2k − 8 = 0. Thus 2k = 4 and k = 2.

23. What is the discriminant of 3x² + x − 2 = 0?

b² − 4ac = 1² − 4(3)(−2) = 1 + 24 = 25.

24. Solve x² + 6x + 8 = 0.

x² + 6x + 8 = (x + 2)(x + 4). Hence x = −2 or −4.

25. How many real roots does x² − 2x + 10 = 0 have?

The discriminant is (−2)² − 4(1)(10) = 4 − 40 = −36. A negative discriminant gives no real roots.
