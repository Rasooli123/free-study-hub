MATHEMATICS 2

Parabola

Chapter 4 · 25 Questions

1. What is the vertex of y = x²?

The basic parabola y = x² has its turning point at the origin.

2. What is the vertex of y = (x − 3)² + 2?

In y = (x − h)² + k, the vertex is (h, k). Here h = 3 and k = 2.

3. What is the axis of symmetry of y = x² − 6x + 5?

The axis is x = −b/(2a) = −(−6)/(2) = 3.

4. Which parabola opens downward?

A parabola opens downward when the coefficient of x² is negative.

5. What are the x-intercepts of y = x² − 4x + 3?

Set y = 0: x² − 4x + 3 = (x − 1)(x − 3).

6. What is the maximum value of y = −x² + 4x + 1?

The vertex is at x = −b/(2a) = 2. Substitution gives y = −4 + 8 + 1 = 5.

7. What is the y-intercept of y = x² + 2x − 3?

At x = 0, y = −3. Therefore the y-intercept is −3.

8. What is the vertex of y = 2(x + 1)² − 8?

x + 1 = x − (−1), so the vertex is (−1, −8).

9. What are the roots of y = (x − 2)² − 9?

Set y = 0: (x − 2)² = 9, so x − 2 = ±3. Thus x = −1 or 5.

10. What is the axis of symmetry of y = 3x² + 12x + 1?

x = −b/(2a) = −12/(6) = −2.

11. The graph y = x² − 10x + 25 touches the x-axis at which x-value?

y = (x − 5)² has a double root at x = 5, so it touches the axis there.

12. Where does y = −x² + 9 meet the x-axis?

Set −x² + 9 = 0. Then x² = 9 and x = ±3.

13. What is the minimum value of y = x² + 4x + 7?

Complete the square: x² + 4x + 7 = (x + 2)² + 3. Its minimum is 3.

14. What is the maximum value of y = −(x + 2)² + 4?

The negative square is at most 0, so the greatest possible y-value is 4.

15. What is the coefficient a in y = 5x² − x + 2?

In y = ax² + bx + c, a is the coefficient of x², which is 5.

16. What is the range of y = (x − 1)² + 6?

The square is at least 0, so y is at least 6.

17. What is the range of y = −(x − 1)² + 6?

Because −(x − 1)² is at most 0, y can be no greater than 6.

18. Find f(2) for f(x) = x² − 3x + 1.

f(2) = 4 − 6 + 1 = −1.

19. Which point lies on y = x² − 1?

For x = 2, y = 2² − 1 = 3, giving the point (2, 3).

20. What is the vertex of y = 2x² − 8x + 3?

x-coordinate: −(−8)/(2·2) = 2. Then y = 2(4) − 16 + 3 = −5.

21. If y = x² + bx + 9 has axis x = −3, what is b?

The axis is x = −b/2. Setting −b/2 = −3 gives b = 6.

22. What are the roots of x² − 2x − 8 = 0?

(x − 4)(x + 2) = 0, so x = 4 or −2.

23. Which graph is the reflection of y = x² across the x-axis?

Reflecting across the x-axis multiplies every y-value by −1, giving y = −x².

24. The graph y = (x + 4)² is shifted how from y = x²?

Replacing x by x + 4 shifts the basic graph 4 units left.

25. If y = ax² passes through (2, 8), what is a?

Substitute (2, 8): 8 = a(2²) = 4a, so a = 2.
