MATHEMATICS 1

Chapter 16: Problems

Test your ability to solve percentage, ratio, age, speed, work, average, profit, and geometry problems.

1. A number is increased by 20% and becomes 72. What was the original number?

Explanation: Let the original number be x. 120% of x = 72, so 1.2x = 72. Therefore, x = 60.

2. Ali has 3 times as many books as Mehmet. Together they have 48 books. How many books does Ali have?

Explanation: Let Mehmet have x books. Ali has 3x books. Therefore, x + 3x = 48 → 4x = 48 → x = 12. Ali has 3 × 12 = 36 books.

3. A shirt costs $80. It is sold with a 25% discount. What is the sale price?

Explanation: 25% of $80 = $20. Therefore, the sale price is $80 − $20 = $60.

4. A car travels 240 km in 4 hours at a constant speed. How far will it travel in 7 hours?

Explanation: Speed = 240 ÷ 4 = 60 km/h. In 7 hours, distance = 60 × 7 = 420 km.

5. The sum of three consecutive integers is 72. What is the largest integer?

Explanation: Let the integers be x, x + 1, and x + 2. Their sum is 72: 3x + 3 = 72 → x = 23. The largest integer is 25.

6. A father is 4 times as old as his son. Their ages add up to 50. How old will the father be when the son is 20?

Explanation: Let the son's age be x. The father's age is 4x. x + 4x = 50 → 5x = 50 → x = 10. The father is currently 40. When the son reaches 20, 10 years will have passed, so the father will be 50.

7. A tank is 3/5 full. After adding 24 liters, it becomes completely full. What is the capacity of the tank?

Explanation: The empty part is 2/5 of the tank. Therefore, 2/5 of the capacity = 24. Capacity = 24 × 5 ÷ 2 = 60 L.

8. A worker earns $15 per hour for the first 8 hours and $22 per hour for each additional hour. How much does the worker earn for 11 hours?

Explanation: First 8 hours: 8 × $15 = $120. Additional 3 hours: 3 × $22 = $66. Total = $120 + $66 = $186.

9. A number is decreased by 30% and then increased by 30%. What percentage of the original number remains?

Explanation: Assume the original number is 100. After a 30% decrease: 100 × 0.70 = 70. After a 30% increase: 70 × 1.30 = 91. Therefore, 91% remains.

10. A train 180 meters long passes a pole in 9 seconds. What is its speed?

Explanation: Speed = distance ÷ time. Speed = 180 ÷ 9 = 20 m/s.

11. The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 3:5. If 4 boys leave and 5 girls join, the ratio becomes 1:2. How many boys were originally in the class?

Explanation: Let the original numbers be 3x boys and 5x girls. After the changes: (3x − 4)/(5x + 5) = 1/2. Therefore, 2(3x − 4) = 5x + 5. 6x − 8 = 5x + 5 → x = 13. Original boys = 3 × 13 = 39.

12. An item is bought for $240 and sold for $300. What is the percentage profit?

Explanation: Profit = $300 − $240 = $60. Profit percentage = (60 ÷ 240) × 100 = 25%.

13. A rectangle has a perimeter of 70 m. Its length is 5 m greater than its width. What is its area?

Explanation: 2(L + W) = 70 → L + W = 35. Since L = W + 5: W + 5 + W = 35 → 2W = 30 → W = 15. Therefore, L = 20. Area = 20 × 15 = 300 m².

14. A rectangle has an area of 180 m². Its length is 3 m greater than its width. What is the width?

Explanation: Let the width be x. The length is x + 3. Therefore: x(x + 3) = 180. Since 12 × 15 = 180, x = 12 m.

15. A student gets 72% of the first 50 questions correct and 80% of the next 50 questions correct. What percentage of all 100 questions did the student answer correctly?

Explanation: First 50: 72% of 50 = 36 correct. Next 50: 80% of 50 = 40 correct. Total correct = 76 out of 100. Therefore, the percentage is 76%.

16. A person spends 3/8 of their money, then spends 1/5 of the remaining money. They have $50 left. How much money did they have originally?

Explanation: After spending 3/8, the person has 5/8 remaining. Then they spend 1/5 of that, leaving 4/5. Remaining money = (5/8) × (4/5) = 1/2 of the original. Since half is $50, the original amount was $100.

17. Two numbers have a sum of 45 and a difference of 9. What is the larger number?

Explanation: Let the larger number be x and the smaller be y. x + y = 45 and x − y = 9. Adding the equations gives 2x = 54. Therefore, x = 27.

18. A tank is 40% full. After adding 30 liters, it becomes 70% full. What is the capacity of the tank?

Explanation: The increase is from 40% to 70%, which is 30% of the tank. Therefore, 30% of the capacity = 30 L. Capacity = 30 ÷ 0.30 = 100 L.

19. The average of 6 numbers is 24. If one number is removed, the average of the remaining 5 numbers becomes 22. What number was removed?

Explanation: Total of 6 numbers = 6 × 24 = 144. Total of remaining 5 = 5 × 22 = 110. Removed number = 144 − 110 = 34.

20. A bus travels 180 km in 3 hours and then 120 km in 2 hours. What is its average speed for the entire journey?

Explanation: Total distance = 180 + 120 = 300 km. Total time = 3 + 2 = 5 hours. Average speed = 300 ÷ 5 = 60 km/h.

21. The average of 4 numbers is 18. If a fifth number is added, the average becomes 22. What is the fifth number?

Explanation: Total of the first 4 numbers = 4 × 18 = 72. Total of 5 numbers = 5 × 22 = 110. Fifth number = 110 − 72 = 38.

22. An item is discounted by 20% and then discounted by another 10%. What is the equivalent total discount?

Explanation: After a 20% discount, 80% remains. After another 10% discount, 90% of that remains. 0.80 × 0.90 = 0.72. Therefore, 72% remains, meaning the total discount is 28%.

23. A product costs $120 after a 20% discount. What was its original price?

Explanation: After a 20% discount, 80% of the original price remains. Therefore, 0.80x = 120. x = 120 ÷ 0.80 = $150.

24. A number is multiplied by 4, then 12 is subtracted. The result is doubled and equals 56. What is the number?

Explanation: Let the number be x. 2(4x − 12) = 56. 4x − 12 = 28. 4x = 40. Therefore, x = 10.

25. A rectangle has a perimeter of 96 m. Its length is twice its width. What is its area?

Explanation: Let the width be x. The length is 2x. Perimeter = 2(L + W) = 96. Therefore: 2(2x + x) = 96 → 6x = 96 → x = 16. Length = 32. Area = 32 × 16 = 512 m².
